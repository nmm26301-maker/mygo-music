# Random playback and listening history, 5.57.0

The existing Android player, package com.mygo.music and playback service are retained. Shuffle now consumes every queue entry once per cycle and avoids immediately choosing the current entry at a cycle boundary. Previous follows the actual listening history; next retraces that history after going back. Entry identity preserves separate same-song entries and cached qualities. Moving queue rows retains the current native player and history. Deleted entries are removed from navigation; newly added entries join the remaining cycle. Toggling shuffle and starting another queue reset its navigation state. Retry, quality replacement and single-track repeat do not create extra history entries.
